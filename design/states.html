Follow views — where the playhead is

Page view. One system fills the lane; the playhead travels left to right. Over the last 18 % of the page the right edge tints to announce the flip; at the end the next system slides in (150 ms) and the playhead restarts after the clef.

Center view. The playhead is fixed at 50 % of the lane width and the systems scroll under it, joined end to end (each system still starts with its clef and key). Past music is on the left, upcoming on the right.

Lane views and themes

Staff, dark (default). Ink --xm-staff-ink on the lane background. Sounding notes take the track colour with a glow; the next expected note in a follow mode gets a dashed amber ring.

Staff, paper. Ink --xm-paper-ink on --xm-paper; the track colour is darkened 35 % for contrast and the playhead loses its glow.

Piano roll, LIVE track. Same melody. Notes of a track you play are outlined (a guide), notes the computer plays are filled. The lead-in gutter shows the key legend where a staff shows clef and key.
