Signal flow — read-only view of the effective routing

Signal flow. Five fixed columns built from the effective setup (base + scene + registers + controller mapping). Coloured = carrying notes right now; dashed = gated by a condition, labelled with it; grey = idle. Clicking a box selects that item in its editor. Nothing is draggable.
